The drawing, and the prohibition
---------------------------------

The cell raster is no longer handed the whole composed interface. `cells::surface_in` classifies an
element as content **only when its whole rect lies inside a declared window content rect**; every
other element is returned exactly as composed, in its own z-order, and the raster's runs are emitted
where the first content element stood. `Mount::content_space()` states the mask from `window_body`,
the same arithmetic the window's own drawing and the pane hit-tests use.

Measured by `shell::cells::tests::the_raster_draws_only_inside_a_window_content_space`:

| element | where it stands | what the raster does |
|---|---|---|
| a bar surface, same paint as the body | outside every content rect | copied through, byte-identical, first in the list |
| a body surface | inside the declared content rect | rasterised to 8 × 16 cell runs |
| a text run | inside the declared content rect | reaches the grid, glyphs present |
| anything above the content space | — | no cell is painted there |
| the whole composed list | no content space declared | returned unchanged — no cells at all |
